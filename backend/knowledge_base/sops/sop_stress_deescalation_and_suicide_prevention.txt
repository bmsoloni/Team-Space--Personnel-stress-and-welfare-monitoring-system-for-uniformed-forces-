STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: STRESS DE-ESCALATION AND CRISIS INTERVENTION
SOP Number: CRPF/SOP/MED-04/REV2

1. CRISIS TRIAGE PROCEDURE
When an automated alert indicates CRITICAL RISK (Stress Score > 75 or Anomaly Flagged):
Step 1: The Welfare Officer and Company Commander receive simultaneous urgent push alerts.
Step 2: Immediate temporary reallocation of the jawan from frontline duties to rear-echelon or administrative tasks.
Step 3: "Buddy Pair" protocol activated — a designated peer is assigned to maintain non-intrusive companionship.
Step 4: Confidential evaluation by Medical Officer within 24 hours.

2. PEER BUDDY SYSTEM RESPONSIBILITIES
Every jawan in combat zones is paired with a Buddy. The Buddy is trained to observe:
- Changes in appetite, hygiene, or sleep patterns.
- Expressions of hopelessness, extreme homesickness, or severe financial anxiety.
- Immediate reporting to the Unit Welfare Officer if weapon safety concerns arise.

3. TELE-COUNSELING AND HELPLINE ACCESS
- Toll-free Paramilitary Mental Health Helpline is operational 24x7 (Confidential & Anonymized).
- Access to civilian clinical psychologists via encrypted video consultation provided at all Group Centers.
