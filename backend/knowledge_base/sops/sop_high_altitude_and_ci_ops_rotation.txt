SOP ON HIGH ALTITUDE AND COUNTER-TERROR ROTATION PROTOCOLS
SOP Number: CRPF/SOP/OPS-11/2024

1. CLIMATE AND ALTITUDE ADAPTATION
- High Altitude deployments above 9,000 feet induce physiological hypoxia, sleep apnea, and elevated cortisol.
- Mandatory acclimatization schedule (Stage I: 6 days, Stage II: 4 days) before active duty.

2. ROTATION SCHEDULES
- Duty shifts in sub-zero sentry posts must not exceed 2 continuous hours without a hot beverage and warm bunker rotation.
- Post-deployment decompression: Units returning from high-intensity counter-terror operations must undergo a 7-day decompression camp emphasizing yoga, sports, and psychological debriefing before redeployment.
